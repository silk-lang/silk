Adds `Pointer.borrowMany` and `Pointer.borrowManyMut`, which make a `&[T]` or `&mut [T]` of `length` elements from a raw many-element pointer. Like `Pointer.borrow`, the result lives as long as the borrow of an anchor argument, and the call compiles to the plain pointer and length pair with no check. New intrinsics `Intrinsic.pointerBorrowMany` and `Intrinsic.pointerBorrowManyMut` back them.

Adds inline clauses on ordinary functions (FUNC-003): `with Intrinsic.inline()` asks the optimizer to prefer inlining the function, `with Intrinsic.inlineAlways()` to inline it into every caller it can, and `with Intrinsic.inlineNever()` never to inline it. They write LLVM's `inlinehint`, `alwaysinline` and `noinline`. The clause on a `static`, `extern` or `export` function or an operation reports SEM0236; one with arguments reports SEM0235.
