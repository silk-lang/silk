A union can name a service declared beside it with `union Opt<T> as service Maybe`, so its name stands for that service in requirement rows, selectors, and `Without`. `never` now conforms to every service, and a provision inside a generic function is decided when `R in Without<R, S>` keeps the selected key out of the rest of the row. This is the compiler side of optional services; the standard library `Option` service follows once the seed parses `as service`.
