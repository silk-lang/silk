Thread pool tasks are fibers: `Pool.fork` returns a `PoolFiber<A, E>` with `await`, `join` and `interrupt`, its outcome is the `Outcome` of `silk.concurrency.fiber`, `Pool.yieldNow` yields, and a wake of a parked fiber is no longer lost when two threads notify it in quick succession.

## Migration

Replace `pool.spawn(task)` with `pool.fork(task)` and `TaskHandle<A>` with `PoolFiber<A, E>`, where `E` is the task's failure type (`never` for a closed task). A task no longer needs `Effect.result`: `join` and `Pool.block` raise its failure unchanged, and both can raise `Cancelled` (import it from `silk.concurrency.fiber`), so add it to the caller's errors.
