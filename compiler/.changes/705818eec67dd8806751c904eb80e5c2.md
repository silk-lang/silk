Replace `Deflate` with the streaming `Encoder`, which adds zlib levels, strategies, flush modes, and dictionaries.

## Migration

Replace `Deflate.encode(input, output, format)` with `Encoder.encode(input, output, format, Options.make(6))` and `Deflate.encodedBound` with `Encoder.encodedBound`. `EncodeError` now has a `kind`; a full buffer is `EncodeErrorKind.OutputFull`.
