Copies a 16-byte union value, such as a tag with a pointer, integer or float payload, as one 128-bit integer instead of a 16-byte `llvm.memmove`. The byte copy also moved the padding after the tag, and LLVM then kept those padding bytes alive through every copy. On the SilkJS interpreter benchmarks this runs 1 to 6.5% fewer instructions on 9 of 11 programs.
