`silk.trace` records a span in about a sixth of the time: records are written in place and timed with the CPU tick counter, and `Config.openCapacity` now counts the records beyond the finished-span buffers (a record is held until a drain takes it); `Recorder.takeSpanInto` and `otlp.encodeInto` drain and encode without copying or measuring twice.
