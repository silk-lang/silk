Make `silk.compress.tar` stream: `TarReader.step` reads an archive in pieces of any size, `TarWriter` writes to the ambient `Writer`, and the new `silk.compress.deflate.EncodeWriter` gzips what a `Writer` gets.

## Migration

- Replace `TarReader.open(archive)` and `TarReader.next` with `TarReader.make()` and a loop over `TarReader.step(&mut reader, input, last)`: advance the input by `progress.consumed`; on `TarStatus.Member` read the header with `TarReader.member(&reader)`; on `TarStatus.Content` the first `consumed` bytes of the input are the file's content; stop on `TarStatus.End`. `TarMember` has no `content` field and gained `size`.
- `TarWriter.file`, `TarWriter.directory` and `TarWriter.finish` now need `? &mut Writer` and fail with `WriterError` instead of `OutOfMemoryError`, and `finish` returns `()`. Provide the destination with `Effect.provideMut<Writer>(&mut destination)`; for a `.tar.gz`, provide an `EncodeWriter`.
