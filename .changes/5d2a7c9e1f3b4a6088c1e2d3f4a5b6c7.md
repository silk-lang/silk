`silk.bench` keeps history: runs record their revision (`--revision`, or the Git commit), `--record` appends to `bench/history/<suite>.jsonl`, `--log` writes the journal `bench/journal.md`, and `--history` shows each suite over time as tables, Markdown, or a page with charts (`silk.bench.history`), and `Format.Bencher` exports Bencher Metric Format.
