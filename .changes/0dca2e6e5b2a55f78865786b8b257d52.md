Accept relative Unix socket paths, and on Linux abstract socket names (a leading NUL byte), in `listenUnix`, `connectUnix`, `connectUnixOwned` and `startConnectUnix`.

## Migration

`InvalidBindReason.RelativePath` and `InvalidEndpointReason.RelativePath` are removed. Delete match arms on them; a relative path now binds or connects relative to the current directory.
