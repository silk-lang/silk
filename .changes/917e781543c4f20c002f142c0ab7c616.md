Make handled failures cheaper under a diagnostic observer: a failure crossing a `run` site now makes one observer call instead of two, because events 1 and 2 of `Intrinsic.observeDiagnostics` take over the reference to `first`.

## Migration

In a custom `Intrinsic.observeDiagnostics` callback, treat `first` of events 1 and 2 as moved into the new node: no event 4 follows for it any more. Stop retaining it, or drop the matching release. `NativeDiagnostics` already does this.
