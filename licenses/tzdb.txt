The time zone bundle in stdlib/silk/time/data/tzdb.bin is generated by scripts/time/generate-tzdb
from release 2026e of the IANA time zone database (the files africa, antarctica, asia,
australasia, europe, northamerica, southamerica, etcetera, and backward of tzdata2026e.tar.gz) and
from the file common/supplemental/windowsZones.xml of release 48 of the Unicode CLDR project. The
license of the CLDR data is in licenses/unicode.txt.
