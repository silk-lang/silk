The transcendental functions in stdlib/silk/num/f64.silk (tan, atan, atan2, asin, acos, exp, log,
pow, cbrt, hypot and fma) are adapted from the math library of musl 1.2.5, src/math. That code
includes fdlibm (tan, atan, atan2, asin, acos, cbrt and the pi/2 reduction) and Arm Optimized
Routines (exp, log and pow, with their tables).
